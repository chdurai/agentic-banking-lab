# Fair Lending Policy (POL-FLN-003)

<!-- meta: version=v1; effectiveFrom=2020-01-01; effectiveTo=2999-12-31; jurisdiction=GLOBAL; product=ALL; tenant=ALL -->

## Section 1: Prohibited Bases (ECOA/Regulation B)
Credit decisions must never consider race, color, religion, national origin, sex,
marital status, age (unless to favor 62+), receipt of public assistance, or the
exercise of consumer rights. Agent reasoning referencing a prohibited basis is a
critical violation.

## Section 2: Adverse Action Notices
Declined applicants must receive specific, accurate principal reasons within 30
days. Generic reasons ("insufficient score") are non-compliant; reasons must be
specific ("debt-to-income ratio of 52% exceeds policy maximum of 50%").

## Section 3: Consistency
Similar applicants must receive similar outcomes. Materially different treatment
of similarly situated applicants triggers a fair-lending review.

## Section 4: Documentation
All decision factors must be documented and retained for 25 months minimum.
