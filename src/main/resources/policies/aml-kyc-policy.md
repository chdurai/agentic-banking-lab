# AML/KYC Policy (POL-AML-002)

<!-- meta: version=v1; effectiveFrom=2020-01-01; effectiveTo=2999-12-31; jurisdiction=GLOBAL; product=ALL; tenant=ALL -->

## Section 1: Sanctions Screening
All applicants must be screened against OFAC SDN and consolidated lists before
any credit decision. A confirmed sanctions match is an automatic decline and must
be reported to the BSA officer within 24 hours. No exceptions.

## Section 2: Politically Exposed Persons
PEP applicants are permitted but require enhanced due diligence (EDD) and senior
management approval. Source-of-funds documentation is mandatory.

## Section 3: Identity Verification
Identity must be verified with two independent sources. Unverifiable identity
results in application suspension, not decline.

## Section 4: Suspicious Activity
Loan purposes that appear designed to layer or structure funds (e.g. vague
"investment" purposes with rapid repayment) must be flagged for a SAR review.
