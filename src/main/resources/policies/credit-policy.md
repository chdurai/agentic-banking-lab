# Retail Credit Policy (POL-CRD-001)

<!-- meta: version=v1; effectiveFrom=2020-01-01; effectiveTo=2999-12-31; jurisdiction=GLOBAL; product=ALL; tenant=ALL -->

## Section 1: Eligibility
Applicants must have verifiable income. Minimum FICO score for unsecured personal
loans is 580. Applicants with an active bankruptcy are ineligible.

## Section 2: Loan Limits
Unsecured personal loans are capped at $100,000 with a maximum term of 84 months.
Loan amounts above $50,000 require two independent income documents.

## Section 3: Debt-to-Income
Total DTI including the proposed payment must not exceed 50%. DTI between 36% and
50% requires senior underwriter approval. DTI below 36% is standard risk.

## Section 4: Pricing
Base APR is 6.5%. Risk premium of 0.1% per risk point applies. Rates must be
disclosed per TILA/Regulation Z before consummation.

## Section 5: Derogatory History
One charge-off older than 24 months may be approved with conditions. Two or more
delinquencies in the last 12 months require manual review.
