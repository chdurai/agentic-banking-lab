# Labor and Working Conditions Grievances (ESF-LAB-001)

<!-- meta: version=v1; effectiveFrom=2020-01-01; effectiveTo=2999-12-31; jurisdiction=GLOBAL; product=ALL; tenant=ALL -->

> Illustrative playbook modeled on common environmental & social practice. Not official policy of any institution.

## Section 1: Typical Issues
Unpaid or late wages, excessive overtime, unsafe working conditions, missing protective
equipment, discrimination in hiring, dismissal without notice, worker accommodation
conditions, subcontractor non-compliance with labor terms.

## Section 2: Assessment Steps
Verify the contractor's labor management procedures and records: contracts, payroll, hours,
incident logs. Check whether the worker is employed by the contractor or a subcontractor and
who holds responsibility. Inspect site conditions where safety is alleged. Interview workers
without management present where possible.

## Section 3: Corrective Actions
Require the contractor to pay arrears with a verifiable payment record, provide protective
equipment immediately, and correct unsafe conditions before work resumes in the affected area.
Document corrective actions with dates and responsible persons and verify on site.

## Section 4: Child and Forced Labor
Any indication of child labor or forced labor is a critical-severity case. Stop the activity,
escalate to a safeguards specialist within 24 hours, and protect the affected persons.
