# Land Acquisition and Resettlement Grievances (ESF-LAR-001)

<!-- meta: version=v1; effectiveFrom=2020-01-01; effectiveTo=2999-12-31; jurisdiction=GLOBAL; product=ALL; tenant=ALL -->

> Illustrative playbook modeled on common environmental & social practice. Not official policy of any institution.

## Section 1: Typical Issues
Disputed compensation valuation, missing or incorrect entitlement records, delays in payment,
loss of livelihood or access to resources, inadequate resettlement housing, disputes over
land titles or customary tenure, forced or inadequately consulted displacement.

## Section 2: Assessment Steps
Check the resettlement action plan and entitlement matrix, the household's records and the
valuation method. Confirm whether the affected person is on the register and whether
customary or informal rights were recognized. Review consultation records.

## Section 3: Corrective Actions
Re-assess valuation where an error is found, correct the register, pay outstanding amounts with
a documented receipt, and provide livelihood restoration support. Offer mediation before
any formal appeal; never proceed with physical displacement while a grievance is unresolved.

## Section 4: Vulnerable Households
Households headed by women, elderly persons, persons with disabilities, and indigenous
communities require additional support and a safeguards specialist review of every decision.
