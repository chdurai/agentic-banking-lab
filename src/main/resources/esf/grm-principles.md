# Grievance Redress Principles (ESF-GRM-001)

<!-- meta: version=v1; effectiveFrom=2020-01-01; effectiveTo=2999-12-31; jurisdiction=GLOBAL; product=ALL; tenant=ALL -->

> Illustrative playbook modeled on common environmental & social grievance-redress practice. Not official policy of any institution.

## Section 1: Intake and Acknowledgement
Every grievance is logged with a unique reference. The complainant receives an acknowledgement
within 3 working days, in a language they understand, stating the reference, the expected
response timeline and how to follow up. Anonymous grievances are accepted and handled on the
same terms; do not require identification or proof as a condition of processing.

## Section 2: Confidentiality and Non-Retaliation
The complainant's identity and account are confidential and shared only on a need-to-know
basis. Any indication of retaliation, intimidation or threats against a complainant or
worker raises the case severity and requires a safeguards specialist to review within 24 hours.
Never ask the complainant to confront the party complained about.

## Section 3: Response and Closure
The response states what was found, what will be done, by whom and by when. The complainant
may accept the outcome or appeal to the next level. A case is closed only when the complainant
has been told the outcome and given the chance to appeal, and the corrective action is verified.

## Section 4: Escalation Criteria
Escalate to a safeguards specialist when a grievance involves serious injury, loss of life,
vulnerable groups, indigenous communities, forced or child labor, or repeated unresolved
complaints about the same issue. Alleged corruption or fraud goes to the confidential case team.
