# Community Health, Safety and Environmental Grievances (ESF-ENV-001)

<!-- meta: version=v1; effectiveFrom=2020-01-01; effectiveTo=2999-12-31; jurisdiction=GLOBAL; product=ALL; tenant=ALL -->

> Illustrative playbook modeled on common environmental & social practice. Not official policy of any institution.

## Section 1: Typical Issues
Dust, noise and vibration from construction, water contamination or reduced water access,
air pollution, waste disposal, heavy-vehicle traffic and road safety near schools, damage to
houses from blasting, disease risks from worker influx.

## Section 2: Assessment Steps
Compare the complaint with the project's environmental and social management plan and permits.
Take measurements where relevant: water quality, dust, noise levels. Check traffic management
plans and incident records. Visit the affected community and speak with local representatives.

## Section 3: Corrective Actions
Apply mitigation in the management plan: dust suppression, restricted working hours, alternative
water supply, route changes away from schools. Repair damage and compensate documented losses.
Report the measures and monitoring results back to the community.

## Section 4: Emergency Conditions
Imminent threat to health or safety, such as contaminated drinking water or structural risk,
is high-severity: act within 24 hours, notify the safeguards specialist and local authorities.
